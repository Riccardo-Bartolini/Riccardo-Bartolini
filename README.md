# Riccardo Bartolini

**M.Sc. Cybersecurity Engineering student · Politecnico di Torino · Turin, Italy**

I am a cybersecurity engineering master's student primarily interested in **network security and governance, risk and compliance (GRC)**. My academic projects have given me practical experience with operating systems, real-time scheduling, authentication and access control.

I am pursuing an English-taught master's degree at Politecnico di Torino, following a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Bologna. My bachelor's thesis explored the cryptographic foundations of Bitcoin. I also gained software development experience during an internship at AREADATI.

## Selected Projects

- **[Sandwich Ordering App](https://github.com/Riccardo-Bartolini/Sandwich-Ordering-App)** — University coursework examining session authentication, user-scoped authorization, server-side validation and TOTP session verification in a React, Express and SQLite application.
- **[Periodic Task Layer for FreeRTOS](https://github.com/Riccardo-Bartolini/PTL-FreeRTOS)** — Team project adding periodic task releases, deadline monitoring and overrun policies to FreeRTOS, with a QEMU demo and regression tests. My contribution focused on the supervisor and scheduling policies.
- **[100 Days War](https://github.com/Riccardo-Bartolini/OOP-100Days_War)** — Java and Swing strategy game developed by a team of four. My work covered the coin system and shop interface, map rendering and tower interactions.
